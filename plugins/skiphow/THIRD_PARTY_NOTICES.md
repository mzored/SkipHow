# Third-party notices

SkipHow includes original adaptations of selected ideas from [`mattpocock/skills`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) at commit `6654f6b60cd9d5be8b54c6fafe44346dabeb3b76`.

The eight adapted SkipHow references are `delegation`, `diagnosis`, `integration`, `product`, `review`, `technical-design`, `testing`, and `tracked-work`. Version 3.0.0 consolidated twenty-three references; version 7.0.0 rewrote them in plain language, split the former `verification` reference into `testing` and `review`, and folded the original `operations` playbook into `integration`, `testing`, `diagnosis`, and `tracked-work`. The owner kernel `SKILL.md` keeps short forms of some of these ideas, including a recommended answer for every owner question, all asked in one round, from the upstream `grilling` skill. The decision frontier itself from `grilling`, asking only what can be put correctly now and asking again after the answers, lives in `product`. The `skiphow-bug`, `skiphow-longrun`, `skiphow-plan`, and `skiphow-simplify` workflows also carry adapted ideas. SkipHow rewrites and narrows the source material for autonomous use by product owners. It does not vendor the upstream skill files. Exact source paths and adapted destination files are recorded in [`SOURCES.json`](SOURCES.json).
