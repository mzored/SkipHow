# Product research brief

Delegate only a bounded question whose answer could change the product decision. Give each researcher:

- the decision to inform;
- the relevant audience and journey;
- the exact sources or product area to inspect;
- a time or evidence boundary;
- the required output: findings, source links or artifact paths, uncertainty, and the decision consequence.

Useful lanes include existing-product behavior, user evidence, analytics, competitors, and established UX patterns. Do not create a lane merely to restate the idea. Prefer current primary evidence. A researcher supplies evidence and does not choose product scope or technical architecture.

For an expensive, hard-to-reverse, or high-blast-radius decision, include one bounded search for evidence that would overturn the leading option. Report what was checked and whether it changed the recommendation. Escalation depends on authority, reversibility, and blast radius, not on a researcher's confidence alone.
