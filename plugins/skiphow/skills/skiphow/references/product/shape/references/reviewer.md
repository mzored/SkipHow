# Independent product review

Use a fresh product reviewer only for an extended decision that is consequential, materially ambiguous, high-impact, difficult to reverse, or required by repository policy. Ordinary clear features rely on scenario evidence from the lightweight brief.

Give the reviewer the decision record, cited evidence, and the minimum existing-product context. Ask for findings about unsupported assumptions, a materially simpler option, missing user states, scope growth, conflicts between sections, or product choices hidden as technical requirements.

Findings must cite the challenged section or evidence and propose a concrete correction. P0 and P1 findings block the decision. P2 findings do not. Reviewers provide evidence and do not set product priority or take Owner authority.
