# Product decisions

Use this reference when a choice changes product behavior, audience, scope, priority, commercial commitments, or risk. Do not turn routine engineering choices into owner questions.

## Find the real decision

State the desired outcome and the smallest unresolved choice that blocks it. Inspect the product, current behavior, repository history, user evidence, and existing decisions first. Research outside the project only when current facts may have changed or the decision carries material cost.

Separate verified facts, assumptions, and preferences. Give a recommendation with its reason and concrete tradeoff. Ask the owner only when evidence cannot settle a product choice or when the action needs explicit human authority.

If the owner does not need to decide now, choose the safest reversible default and record the assumption. A read-only decision request does not authorize implementation.

## Keep the result useful

Record the outcome, acceptance criteria, excluded scope, unresolved risk, and what evidence would trigger a review. Avoid a long specification when a short decision is enough.
