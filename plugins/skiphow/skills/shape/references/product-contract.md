# Product Contract

Write the approved product decision in this form. Keep each section concrete and brief.

```text
Outcome
The user or business result that should improve.

User and trigger
Who encounters this and in what situation.

Required behavior
What the user must be able to do or understand.

Journey
The main path and material alternative, empty, error, and recovery states.

Scope
What this decision includes.

Non-goals
What it intentionally excludes.

Success
An observable signal, baseline when known, measurement window, and guardrails.

Delivery acceptance
Whether Product Director acceptance is required after implementation, the contract scenarios to check, and the evidence that can prove them.

Product constraints
Business, policy, market, accessibility, or user constraints only.

Evidence and assumptions
What the evidence establishes and what remains a hypothesis.
```

Do not prescribe technical implementation. Record product priority and its reason next to the contract in the canonical tracker.
